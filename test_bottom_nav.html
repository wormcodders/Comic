🧪 Bottom Navigation Test Page

Instructions

1. On Mobile/Tablet: Swipe up on the handle at the bottom to open the navigation

2. On Desktop: Click or drag the handle to open

3. Keyboard: Press Tab to focus the handle, then Enter/Space to toggle

4. Escape: Press Escape to close the navigation
